In Progress

Prioritized Completion Checklist (15–18 Nov)

  1. 5-Minute Countdown & Timeout: Starts at 5:00 paused, freezes on pause, loss at 0:00; Esc cannot restart an ended round.
  2. Map Boundaries: Player confined to playable map; geese spawn inside; both hidden events reachable.
  3. Two Usable Starting Items: BreadCrumbs + second usable starting item from requirements (distinct from LongBoiStatue BusTicket reward).
  4. Goose Dispersal & Victory: Dispersal gameplay effect + clear win condition before timeout, reachable with available items/spawns.
  5. Two One-Time Hidden Events: Expand Long Boi proximity dialogue into Event 1 + add Event 2 (both hidden until triggered with observable results).
  6. Two Non-Duplicating Counters: Track items used and unique hidden events triggered (reopening one-time dialogue leaves count unchanged).
